Where your devices can reach this Synology

Devices find this Synology through Syncthing's global discovery servers and connect directly, through your router (NAT traversal) or, if nothing else works, through Syncthing's public relays. Everything is encrypted end to end; relays and discovery servers see that two devices talk, not what they sync. Nothing leaves your network: no global discovery, relays or NAT traversal, no usage reports. Devices sync only when they're at home, found by local discovery or by their address.
